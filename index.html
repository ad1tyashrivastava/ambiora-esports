How the tournament works

From sign-up to the trophy in four steps.

  1. 1

    Register

    Create your team and add five players with their in-game names and roles.

  2. 2

    Fixtures drop

    A round-robin schedule is generated, so every team faces every other team once.

  3. 3

    League stage

    Five rounds, ten matches. A win is worth 3 points and a draw 1.

  4. 4

    Grand final

    The top two teams on the points table play one match for the title.

Questions

How many players can a team have?

Exactly five. Every player needs a name, an in-game name, a role and a game ID.

Can one player join two teams?

No. In-game names and game IDs must be unique across the whole tournament.

What happens when there are an odd number of teams?

Each round one team gets a bye and rests. With five teams, every team rests exactly once.

How is a tie on points broken?

By score difference first, then by total score.
